# Competitor Research

Researched October 2026 from App Store listings. Ratings and prices change often.

## Bible apps for women (the model we are matching)

| App | Rating | Model | Main features | Main complaints |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bible For Women (Pandas of Caribbean) | 4.9 · 254K ratings · #8 Books | Ads, plus a subscription to remove them ($1.99/week to $49.99/year) | Daily verse, morning and night prayer reminders, devotionals, audio, notes, colored highlights, bookmarks, quizzes, offline, reading progress | Intrusive game ads, progress lost, audio not matching the chosen version, tracking (contact info and identifiers) |
| Women's Bible: Verse & Prayer (Rocketshield) | 4.9 · 120K ratings · #16 Books | $2.99/month or $26.99/year, ads | Morning and evening verses and prayers, notes, highlights, bookmarks, night mode, sharing, quiz | Ads, a rating prompt right away, lock screen pop-ups, a gender question at sign up |

## Quran and Muslim apps for women

| App | Traction | What it has |
|---|---|---|
| Quran for Muslim Women (Benoit Reverte) | No ratings yet | Verses by mood, morning, evening and night duas, topics such as Women in Islam, quizzes, daily goals. $4.99/week or $9.99/month |
| Quran For Her (AFRR LLC) | 1 rating | Quran reader, prayer times (Sunni or Shia), prayer log with streaks, recitation, favorites, daily goal, iCloud sync. Contains ads |
| Qariah | 4.9 · 234 ratings | 60+ women reciters with ijaza, several qira'at, ayah repeat, offline audio. Asks for login |
| Quran With Maryam | 16 ratings | Recitation by women reciters |
| Nisa Care | Under 1K downloads | Period tracking with prayer exemptions, qada counter, fasting log, tasbih, duas, journal |
| MyHayd, Sila, Tuhr, Ayda | Small | Hayd tracking by madhhab. Tuhr is fully on device |
| Muslimah | Small | Women-friendly prayer space finder, salah tracker, articles |

**Gap:** no women's Quran app has real scale. The Bible apps prove the demand. The Muslim apps each do one slice, and most rely on ads, streaks, or logins.

## Still missing (next steps)

1. Women reciters. The most loved feature in this space (Qariah). Needs licensing agreements with reciters.
2. Tafsir. Needs a licensed source.
3. Word by word translation and memorization mode.
4. More UI languages (Urdu, Indonesian, Turkish, Arabic, French).
5. A clear business model. Recommendation: free core, with an optional yearly "Supporter" purchase for extras such as reciters, themes and widgets. No ads.
