Quran for Women

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy explains what the Quran for Women app does with your information. The short version: we do not collect any of it.

  • There is no account and no sign in.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • What you save and write stays on your iPhone.
  • We, the people who make the app, never receive your data. We have no server that could receive it.

What stays on your iPhone

The app keeps these things on your iPhone only:

  • Your saved verses, highlights and verse notes
  • Your saved duas and your own personal prayers
  • Your reading place, reading progress and reading plan
  • Your prayer log, rest days and dhikr counts
  • Your journal pages and lesson notes
  • Your settings, such as language, text size, reciter and reminder times
  • The translations you download

Your journal is stored in a file that iOS encrypts while your iPhone is locked.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud Sync in You > Settings > iCloud Sync, the app copies your saved verses, highlights, verse notes, saved duas, personal prayers, reading place, reading plan, prayer log, rest days and dhikr counts to your own iCloud account. This lets you see them on your other Apple devices.

This data goes to your iCloud, under your Apple Account. We cannot see it. Apple's privacy policy covers how iCloud stores it.

Your journal pages and lesson notes are not synced. They stay on the one iPhone.

Sync is off until you turn it on.

Location

If you set up prayer times, the app asks for your location. It uses it to work out prayer times and the Qibla direction. The calculation happens on your iPhone. Your location is saved on your iPhone so prayer times keep working.

To show the name of your city, iOS asks Apple's location service to turn your position into a place name. This is part of iOS. We do not receive your location.

You can say no, or turn location off later in the iPhone Settings app. Prayer times and Qibla will then not work.

Microphone and speech recognition

When you record a lesson in Journal > Notes, the app uses the microphone and speech recognition to write out what the speaker says. This only happens while you are recording.

The audio is not saved. Only the text is kept, as a note on your iPhone.

On most recent iPhones, speech recognition runs on the iPhone itself. On iPhones that cannot do this, iOS may send the audio to Apple to turn it into text. This is Apple's own service, covered by Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive the audio or the text.

Face ID

You can lock your journal with Face ID or your passcode. Face ID is handled by iOS. The app only learns whether you unlocked it. It never sees your face data. The lock is off until you turn it on.

Notifications

The app can send you a daily reminder to read, an evening reminder for duas and dhikr, and a reminder at each prayer time. These are set up on your iPhone. Nothing is sent from a server. You choose which ones you want, and you can turn them off in the app or in the iPhone Settings app.

Downloads and audio from the internet

The app connects to the internet for two things only:

  • Translations and transliteration. When you choose a translation, or turn on transliteration, the app downloads it once from AlQuran Cloud (api.alquran.cloud). After that it works offline.
  • Recitation. When you play a verse, the audio streams from Islamic Network (cdn.islamic.network). If you download a surah to listen offline, its audio comes from the same place once and is then kept on your phone.

These requests only say which translation or which verse and reciter you want. Like any website, these services can see your IP address when your iPhone connects to them. We do not send them any personal information, any ID or anything you have written. They are separate services with their own policies.

The Arabic Quran text, the duas, the dhikr and the daily verses are built into the app and need no internet.

Sharing

If you share a verse, a dua, a journal page or a note, the app hands it to the iPhone share sheet. You choose where it goes. The app does not send it anywhere by itself.

Deleting your data

  • In You > Settings > Privacy & About, Erase My Data deletes your saved verses, highlights, verse notes, saved duas, personal prayers, reading progress, reading plan, prayer log, rest days, dhikr counts, journal pages and lesson notes from this iPhone, and from your iCloud if sync is on.
  • You can also delete journal pages and lesson notes one at a time in the Journal tab.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone.

On Android phones

Everything above applies to the Android app too, with these differences:

  • Backup instead of iCloud sync. There is no sync switch. If backup is on in your phone's settings, Android copies your saved verses, highlights, notes, saved duas, personal prayers, reading progress, prayer log, dhikr counts and settings into your own Google backup, encrypted so only your phone can read it. Your journal pages and lesson notes are never included. Downloaded translations and recitations are not included either; they download again.
  • City name. To show the name of your city, Android's own location service turns your position into a place name. This is part of Android. We do not receive your location. The app only asks for your approximate location, never your precise one.
  • Lesson notes. Speech is written out by Android's own speech recognition, on the phone where it can. If your phone has no offline speech model, Android's recognition service may process the audio. The app never saves the audio.
  • Journal lock. The journal can be locked with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Android handles this; the app only learns whether you unlocked it.
  • Downloads. Recitations you download to listen offline are saved on your phone and are not backed up.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts to handle data in a new way, we will update this policy and change the effective date above before that version is released.
