Support

Help with the app, and answers to common questions.

About the app

Quran for Women is an iPhone and Android app for reading the Quran, making dua and remembering Allah each day. It has the full Arabic Quran built in, translations in 13 languages, verse by verse recitation, duas with their sources, a dhikr counter, a private journal, lesson notes, prayer times and the Qibla.

There is no account, no ads and no tracking. What you save and write stays on your phone. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

My prayer times do not match my mosque. Why?

The app works out prayer times on your phone from your location, using a calculation method. Mosques and countries use different methods, mainly for Fajr and Isha, so times can differ by a few minutes or more.

To fix it, go to You > Settings > Prayer Times and:

  • Choose the Method your local mosque uses.
  • Choose Asr time: Standard (Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali) or Hanafi. Hanafi Asr is later.
  • If you have moved or are travelling, tap Update next to your location.

If you are not sure, ask your mosque which method its timetable uses, and always follow your local mosque when in doubt.

Which calculation method should I choose?

The app picks one for your country the first time it finds your location. You can change it at any time. These are the choices:

  • Muslim World League: used in much of Europe and the Far East.
  • North America (ISNA): United States and Canada.
  • Egyptian General Authority: Egypt, much of Africa, Syria, Iraq, Lebanon and nearby.
  • Umm al-Qura, Makkah: Saudi Arabia and some Gulf countries.
  • University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi: Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Afghanistan.
  • Dubai: United Arab Emirates.
  • Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia.
  • Diyanet, Turkey.

In places far north or south, the sky may not get fully dark in summer. The app then keeps Fajr and Isha within a part of the night. Your local mosque's timetable is the best guide there.

What are rest days?

Rest days are for the days of your period, when prayer is paused. Turn on Rest days on the Prayer Times screen. While it is on, prayer reminders stop and the prayer log is paused. Turn it off when you are ready to pray again.

The app does not track your cycle and gives no rulings. For questions of fiqh, please ask a scholar you trust.

How do I lock my journal?

Go to the Journal tab, tap the options button, then Journal Settings. Turn on Lock with Face ID or passcode. You can also find it in You > Settings > Journal.

The journal locks when you leave the app. If Face ID is not set up, your iPhone passcode unlocks it. You can also hide page previews in the journal list.

On Android, the lock uses your fingerprint, face or screen lock.

The lock is off until you turn it on.

How does iCloud sync work?

Go to You > Settings > iCloud Sync and turn on Sync with iCloud. Do this on each iPhone you use, signed in to the same Apple Account.

It syncs your saved verses, highlights, verse notes, saved duas, personal prayers, reading place, reading plan, prayer log, rest days and dhikr counts. The data goes to your own iCloud. We cannot see it.

Your journal pages and lesson notes are not synced. They stay on the iPhone where you wrote them. You can export them as a text file from Journal Settings.

If you change the same thing on two devices, the most recent change is kept.

On Android there is no iCloud. Your saved items and settings can be included in your own Google backup. The journal is never included.

Where do the Quran text, translations and recitation come from?
  • Arabic text: the Tanzil Project, Uthmani script. It is built into the app, unchanged, and works offline.
  • Translations: from AlQuran Cloud. Choose one in You > Settings > Language & Translation. It downloads once, then works offline. You can delete downloads in You > Settings > Downloads.
  • Recitation: streamed verse by verse from Islamic Network. Reciters are Mishary Rashid Alafasy, Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary and Mohamed Siddiq Al-Minshawi.
  • Duas: duas from the Quran are taken from the built in Arabic text. Duas from hadith show their collection and number.

No verse or hadith in the app is written or changed by AI. Your own prayers are kept in Your Own Words and are always labeled as personal.

I do not get prayer notifications.

Check that notifications for Quran for Women are allowed in the iPhone Settings app. In the app, go to You > Settings > Prayer Times > Prayer settings and make sure Notify at prayer times is on and the prayers you want are chosen. Notifications are paused while Rest days is on. On Android, also allow Alarms & reminders for the app so reminders arrive on time. Open the app at least once every few days so it can set up the next days of reminders.

Is the lesson recording saved?

No. When you record a khutbah or class in Journal > Notes, the app writes out what is said and keeps only the text. The audio is never saved.

How do I delete my data?

In You > Settings > Privacy & About, tap Erase My Data. This removes your saved items, highlights, notes on verses, personal prayers, progress, plan, prayer log, dhikr counts, journal pages and lesson notes. Deleting the app removes everything.
