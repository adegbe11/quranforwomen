Using the app

You may use the app for free for your own reading, worship and learning, and share verses and duas from it with family and friends.

The Quran text and translations

The Arabic text of the Quran is the Uthmani text from the Tanzil Project, shown without changes. Translations, word meanings, tafsir and recitations belong to their translators, scholars and reciters. The app brings them to you through AlQuran Cloud, Quran.com and Islamic Network, and names the source where you read them.

We take great care that every verse and dua is correct. If you ever see a mistake, please tell us at the email below so we can fix it quickly.

Not religious rulings

The app is a companion for reading, dua and remembrance. It does not give fatwas or rulings. For questions of fiqh, please ask a qualified scholar you trust.

Prayer times and the Qibla are worked out on your phone from your location and the method you choose. They can differ a little from your local mosque. When in doubt, follow your local masjid.
